Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data OmniNode handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we handle

  • Account data: your email, organisation name, owner name, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and login dates.
  • Billing data: your invoices, plan, chosen payment method and payment confirmations. We don't collect card numbers.
  • Payload data: the content of webhooks and messages you send through OmniNode, and the mapping rules and sample data you save.
  • Destination credentials: the API keys or tokens you give us so we can deliver data to your systems.
  • Technical data: IP addresses and request details in server logs, used for security and troubleshooting.

2. Our role

For account and billing data, we decide how it's used. For payload data, we act on your behalf: we process it only to receive, transform, deliver, log and replay it as you configure. If you're an agency, you are responsible for your clients' payload data.

3. How we use data

  • to provide the Service: receive, transform, deliver and log your events;
  • to manage billing and send service emails such as renewal reminders and password resets;
  • to provide support, keep the Service secure and prevent abuse;
  • to improve the Service using aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics.

We don't sell your data and we don't use payload data for advertising.

4. AI processing

Only messages you send to the AI parsing endpoint are processed by AI. They are sent to our AI model provider solely to extract structured fields and return the result. We don't use your data to train AI models.

5. Storage and retention

  • Webhook payloads are stored so they can be delivered, retried, shown in your logs and replayed. They're kept while your account exists, unless a plan states a shorter log period.
  • When your account is deleted, its workspaces, endpoints, credentials, events and users are permanently deleted from our active database. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.
  • We keep invoice records as long as needed for accounting and legal obligations.

6. Security

  • Connections to the app and API use HTTPS.
  • Passwords and OmniNode API keys are stored as one-way hashes and can't be read back, even by us.
  • Every workspace is isolated: one customer can't access another's endpoints, mappings, credentials or events.
  • Destination credentials must be usable to call your systems, so they're stored in our database with access restricted to the Service and our administrators.

7. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run OmniNode: our cloud hosting provider (servers and database), an AI model provider (only for AI parsing), an email provider (service emails), Google Fonts (website fonts), and an internal messaging tool our team uses for operational alerts, which may include an account name, email or invoice reference. These providers may be located outside Jordan.

8. Browser storage

The website and app use your browser's local storage for your language choice, your login session and app preferences. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your profile and email in Settings.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account by contacting us. We'll reply within 30 days.
