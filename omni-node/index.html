How it works

From "new event" to "delivered" in three steps

Set up a connection in minutes. Every event after that is handled for you.

Get your inbound URL

Create a source and OmniNode gives you a private webhook URL. Paste it into Shopify, your form tool, your payment gateway, or any system that sends webhooks. Shopify signatures can be verified too.

Map the fields visually

Paste a sample of the incoming data, or start from a template. Tap a field on the left, then the field it belongs to on the right. Set types and simple math, and preview the exact output before you save.

Deliver and monitor

Each event is accepted instantly, queued, transformed and sent to your destination. If it fails, OmniNode retries automatically, keeps the event in a failed queue and lets you replay it with one click.

AI message parsing

Messages in. Structured data out.

Customers don't fill forms; they send messages. OmniNode's AI reads free-text messages in Arabic or English and extracts clean fields your systems can use.

      Built for reliability
      
Every event caught. Every failure visible.

      
The hard part of integrations isn't the first request. It's what happens when something goes wrong.

    

    

      
Accepted instantly, never dropped
Webhooks are saved to a queue the moment they arrive and the sender gets an immediate response, even if your destination is slow or down.

      
Automatic retries
Failed deliveries are retried automatically, up to 5 attempts with growing waits in between, so short outages fix themselves.

      
Failed queue & one-click replay
Anything that still fails waits in a failed-events queue with the exact error. Fix the cause, then replay one event or all of them.

      
Visual mapping studio
Link fields by tapping, set text/number/boolean/date types, apply simple math and preview the output. Nested fields are supported.

      
Schema discovery & suggestions
Paste one sample payload and OmniNode reads its structure, even from undocumented in-house systems, and suggests matching fields for you.

      
Secure by design
Private secret URLs, Shopify signature checks, API keys stored as one-way hashes and strict isolation between workspaces.

      
Logs, usage & alerts
See every event with its payload and status, track usage against your plan, and get warned at 80% and 100% before anything stops.

      
Ready-made templates
Start from built-in sample schemas instead of a blank page, then adjust the mapping to your exact needs.

      
Developer API
Endpoints, mappings, webhooks and event logs are available over a documented REST API with API-key authentication.

FAQ
Questions, answered

    

      
Do I need a developer?
No. You paste the inbound URL into your source app and map fields visually in the dashboard. A developer only helps if the system you're connecting has no webhook settings, or if you want to use our API directly.

      
What counts as a task?
Each incoming webhook accepted by OmniNode is one task, no matter how many fields it has. Messages sent to the AI parser also count as one AI task. Retries of the same event don't use extra tasks.

      
What happens if my destination is down?
The event stays safely queued. OmniNode makes up to 5 delivery attempts with increasing waits. If it still fails, it moves to the failed-events queue with the exact error, and you can replay it once the destination is back.

      
What happens when I reach my monthly limit?
You're warned at 80% and 100%. You then get a 10% grace buffer so nothing stops abruptly. Above that, new webhooks are refused until you upgrade or the next month starts.

      
How do I pay?
Choose a plan in the app and generate an invoice. Pay by CliQ, bank transfer or cash using the reference shown, then tap "I've paid". Your plan is activated as soon as the payment is confirmed.

      
What happens after the free trial?
After 14 days, incoming webhooks pause until you choose a plan. Your setup, connections and logs are kept, so you continue right where you left off.

      
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Plans are monthly. If you don't renew, your account moves to a short grace period and is then paused. There's nothing to cancel and no long-term contract.

      
Is my data used to train AI?
No. Only messages you send to the AI parsing endpoint are processed by our AI provider, only to extract fields. We don't use your data to train models. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

      
Can I connect a system that isn't listed?
Yes. If it can send an HTTP request with JSON, it can be a source. If it accepts an HTTP request, it can be a destination. Paste a sample payload and OmniNode reads its structure.

      
I'm an agency. How do client accounts work?
You manage everything: create a workspace per client, give each one a share of your pooled tasks, and build their connections yourself. Clients don't need their own logins. You can send them branded PDF reports.
